TTReferee 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日 · 适用于 TTReferee iOS 1.1

TTReferee 由 WeiyePlayer 开发。应用在设备本地处理比赛录像，不设置账号，不包含广告、行为统计或第三方追踪，不将录像、标点、设置或判罚结果发送至开发者的服务器。

1. 在设备上处理的信息

应用在你授予相机权限、处于拍摄主页时采集视频，用于滚动录像和人工复核。录像不录制声音，不请求麦克风权限。近期录像、复核帧、标点和证据生成过程在设备上处理。应用在本地保存语言、镜头、画质、帧率和保存完整录像的偏好。

2. 权限与用途

  • 相机：拍摄用于发球复核的画面。未授予权限时无法拍摄，可在系统设置中随时撤回。
  • 添加照片：仅在你选择保存证据或开启保存完整挑战录像时，将 JPEG、MP4、GIF 或挑战录像添加到系统照片库。应用不通过照片权限浏览或读取你的照片库。

拒绝保存权限不阻止人工复核；保存操作将提示失败。权限可以在 iOS 设置中管理。

3. 保存期限与删除

滚动录像以临时片段保存，最多保留约三分钟，旧片段按整段淘汰。挑战时冻结近期录像；继续比赛后清理上一轮临时挑战文件。应用启动时清理过期临时文件。文件正在预览或导出时，清理会延后到使用结束。

已保存的照片和视频归系统照片库管理。请在“照片”中删除；卸载 TTReferee 不会删除照片库或已分享出去的副本。卸载应用会移除应用内的设置和本地临时文件。

4. 分享与第三方服务

只有在你主动打开系统分享入口并选择接收方后，证据才会按你的操作交给该应用或服务；接收方的处理受其隐私政策约束。请尊重画面中人员的隐私，并在必要时取得拍摄或分享许可。

打开公开隐私政策、支持网页或提交反馈会访问 GitHub。GitHub Pages 提供本网站托管；GitHub 可能按其隐私声明处理网站请求和反馈。本站不嵌入统计、广告、追踪脚本或第三方字体。GitHub Issues 为公开渠道，不应提交私人录像、运动员照片或个人联系方式。

5. 联系与政策更新

开发者：WeiyePlayer。如有隐私问题，请通过支持仓库 GitHub Issues联系。我们不持有应用中的本地比赛数据，因此数据删除请按上述设备端步骤操作。

功能或数据处理方式变化时，会更新此页面的生效日期和应用内政策。当前版本没有账号注册、服务器上传或订阅服务。

TTReferee Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · TTReferee iOS 1.1

TTReferee is developed by WeiyePlayer. It processes match footage on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not send recordings, points, preferences or review results to a developer-operated server.

Local processing and permissions

With your camera permission, TTReferee captures video on the recording screen for a rolling recording and manual review. It records no audio and requests no microphone permission. Recent footage, extracted frames, marked points and evidence generation are processed locally. Language, camera, resolution, frame-rate and full-recording preferences are stored locally.

Camera permission is required for capture. Add-only Photos permission is used when you save evidence or enable saving full challenge recordings. It allows JPEG, MP4, GIF or full challenge video to be added to Photos. TTReferee does not browse or read your photo library through this permission. Refusing export permission does not prevent manual review. Manage or revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

The rolling recording holds up to about three minutes in temporary segments and evicts older whole segments. Starting a challenge freezes recent footage; continuing play clears the previous temporary challenge files. Expired temporary files are cleaned on app launch. Files in active preview or export remain until that use ends.

Saved photos and videos are managed by the system Photos library and must be deleted in Photos. Uninstalling TTReferee removes the app's preferences and temporary files, but does not delete saved or shared copies.

Sharing and external services

Evidence is handed to a recipient app or service only when you initiate system sharing and choose that recipient. Its privacy policy applies to that processing. Respect the privacy of people in the footage and obtain recording or sharing consent when needed.

Opening public policy/support pages or submitting feedback connects to GitHub. GitHub hosts this site through GitHub Pages and may process requests and feedback under its privacy statement. This site has no analytics, advertisements, tracking scripts or third-party fonts. GitHub Issues are public: do not submit private footage, player photos or personal contact details.

Contact and changes

Developer: WeiyePlayer. Contact us about privacy through GitHub Issues. We do not hold your local match data; use the device deletion steps above. We will update the effective date and the in-app policy if functionality or data handling changes. This version has no account registration, server upload or subscriptions.
